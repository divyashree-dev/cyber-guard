Before You Pay Safety Checklist

💡 Golden Myth Buster Rule:

You NEVER enter a UPI PIN to RECEIVE money into your account.

Entering your UPI PIN ALWAYS deducts funds from your account. If a buyer on OLX/Marketplace asks you to enter your PIN to receive an advance, IT IS 100% A FRAUD!

🚫 Rule #1: Never share OTP, UPI PIN, or CVV

No genuine representative will ever request confidential credentials.

🚫 Rule #2: Do not scan QR codes to receive money

QR codes are strictly used to MAKE payments, not receive them.

🚫 Rule #3: Never share screen during money transfer

Disconnect remote access tools (AnyDesk, QuickSupport) before opening banking apps.
